# AnythingScreensaver
Ya' know those DVD screensavers that have the DVD logo move on the screen?\
What if... this, but you can have whatever you want as the image? Just upload your picture of choice and.. have fun!

## Privacy Notice
Any image uploaded on AnythingScreensaver will (obviously) stay on your device for as long as you don't close/reload the page. Nothing is sent to any servers.
